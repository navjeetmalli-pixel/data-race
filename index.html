Data Race Uploader

Data Race Uploader is a personal tool used by the owner of the Data Race YouTube channel. It creates animated data-visualization videos (bar-chart races and country comparisons) from World Bank Open Data (CC BY 4.0) and uploads them to that one channel through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.
